# Business associate agreement template

> **TEMPLATE: requires legal review before signature.** This is a working draft for the agreement
> between FDHS Westchase Gastroenterology (the covered entity) and the business associate that
> builds and operates the patient website and staff portal. It is not legal advice and is not in
> force. Counsel licensed in Florida reviews it, completes the bracketed items and adds the
> contract formalities before either party signs.

## About this template

The agreement text below adapts HHS's
[Sample Business Associate Agreement Provisions](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/sample-business-associate-agreement-provisions/index.html)
(published January 25, 2013) to this system. HHS states that the sample provisions address only
the HIPAA Rules, may not by themselves form a binding contract under state law, and do not replace
a lawyer's advice or the parties' negotiation. That is why counsel's review is a precondition.

The template carries every term that
[45 CFR 164.504(e)(2) to (e)(4)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.504#p-164.504(e)(2))
and [164.314(a)(2)(i)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.314#p-164.314(a)(2)(i))
require:

| Required term | Source | Section below |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permitted and required uses and disclosures | 164.504(e)(2)(i) | 3 |
| No other use or disclosure | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(A) | 2(a) |
| Safeguards and compliance with the Security Rule | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(B); 164.314(a)(2)(i)(A) | 2(b) |
| Report non-permitted uses and disclosures, breaches of unsecured PHI and security incidents | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(C); 164.314(a)(2)(i)(C); [164.410](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.410) | 2(c) |
| Subcontractors agree to the same restrictions, conditions and Security Rule requirements | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(D); 164.314(a)(2)(i)(B); [164.502(e)(1)(ii)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.502#p-164.502(e)(1)(ii)); [164.308(b)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.308#p-164.308(b)(2)) | 2(d) |
| Access, amendment and accounting of disclosures | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(E) to (G) | 2(e) to 2(g) |
| Privacy Rule obligations carried out for the covered entity | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(H) | 2(h) |
| Books and records available to the Secretary | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(I) | 2(i) |
| Return or destruction at termination, or extended protection where infeasible | 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J) | 6(c) |
| Termination by the covered entity for a material violation | 164.504(e)(2)(iii) | 6(b) |
| Management and administration uses, if permitted | 164.504(e)(4) | 3(e), 3(f) |

Section 2(c) sets the same reporting deadlines as the business associate's
[incident response policy](policies/incident-response.md#notifying-the-covered-entity), including
the 10-day notice a third-party agent owes under Florida's
[section 501.171(6)(a)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0501/Sections/0501.171.html).
A change to either one changes the other in the same pull request.

### For counsel to decide or complete

- The contracting legal entity on each side, their addresses and authorized signatories.
- The effective date and the service agreement this agreement attaches to.
- Whether the business associate may use PHI for its own management and administration
  (sections 3(e) and 3(f)), and if so, whether termination option 6(c)(2) applies.
- The cure period for termination for cause (section 6(b)).
- Response times for access, amendment and accounting requests (sections 2(e) to 2(g)).
- Who sends breach notices to individuals, HHS and the media, and who bears their cost
  (section 2(c)(7)).
- Whether Florida law or the parties' practice calls for further terms: indemnification,
  insurance, limitation of liability, governing law and venue.
- How section 501.171(4)(g) applies to the covered entity's own notice deadline, as noted in the
  [incident response policy](policies/incident-response.md#notifications-the-covered-entity-makes).

Words in square brackets are either choices for the parties or text to insert.

**BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT**

This Business Associate Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] by
FDHS Westchase Gastroenterology [CONFIRM CONTRACTING LEGAL ENTITY] ("Covered Entity") and
[BUSINESS ASSOCIATE LEGAL NAME] ("Business Associate"), each a "Party."

## 1. Definitions

(a) **Catch-all definition.** The following terms used in this Agreement have the same meaning as
those terms in the HIPAA Rules: Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure,
Electronic Protected Health Information, Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary,
Notice of Privacy Practices, Protected Health Information, Required By Law, Secretary, Security
Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and Use. "PHI" means Protected
Health Information that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf
of Covered Entity, and includes Electronic Protected Health Information.

(b) **Business Associate.** "Business Associate" has the same meaning as the term "business
associate" at 45 CFR 160.103 and, in reference to the Party to this Agreement, means
[BUSINESS ASSOCIATE LEGAL NAME].

(c) **Covered Entity.** "Covered Entity" has the same meaning as the term "covered entity" at
45 CFR 160.103 and, in reference to the Party to this Agreement, means FDHS Westchase
Gastroenterology [CONFIRM CONTRACTING LEGAL ENTITY].

(d) **HIPAA Rules.** "HIPAA Rules" means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and
Enforcement Rules at 45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164.

(e) **Services.** "Services" means the services Business Associate provides to Covered Entity
under [TITLE AND DATE OF THE SERVICE AGREEMENT] (the "Service Agreement"): building, hosting,
operating, securing, and supporting Covered Entity's patient website, its appointment-request
intake, and the staff portal Covered Entity's workforce uses to work those requests.

(f) **Unsuccessful Security Incident.** "Unsuccessful Security Incident" means an attempted
Security Incident that does not result in unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or
destruction of PHI or interference with the operation of the information system, such as pings
and other broadcast attacks on firewalls, port scans, unsuccessful sign-in attempts, requests
blocked by a firewall, and denial-of-service attempts that do not affect availability.

## 2. Obligations and Activities of Business Associate

Business Associate agrees to:

(a) Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement or as Required
By Law.

(b) Use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 with respect to
Electronic Protected Health Information, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as
provided for by this Agreement.

(c) Report to Covered Entity as follows:

1. **Breaches.** Any Breach of Unsecured Protected Health Information, as required at
   45 CFR 164.410, and any breach of security of personal information under section 501.171,
   Florida Statutes, without unreasonable delay and in no case later than ten (10) calendar days
   after the Breach is discovered (as 45 CFR 164.410(a)(2) defines discovery) or after Business
   Associate determines, or has reason to believe, that it occurred.
2. **Other non-permitted uses and disclosures.** Any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by
   this Agreement of which Business Associate becomes aware, within the same ten (10) calendar
   days.
3. **Security Incidents.** Any Security Incident of which Business Associate becomes aware, other
   than an Unsuccessful Security Incident, within the same ten (10) calendar days.
4. **Unsuccessful Security Incidents.** This paragraph is notice to Covered Entity that
   Unsuccessful Security Incidents occur continually, and no further report of them is required.
   Business Associate provides a summary of them on Covered Entity's written request.
5. **Content.** A Breach report includes, to the extent known and supplemented as Business
   Associate learns more: the identification of each Individual whose Unsecured Protected Health
   Information has been, or is reasonably believed by Business Associate to have been, accessed,
   acquired, used, or disclosed (45 CFR 164.410(c)(1)); a description of what happened, the date
   of the Breach and the date of discovery; the types of PHI involved; what Business Associate is
   doing to investigate, mitigate harm, and prevent recurrence; Business Associate's risk
   assessment under 45 CFR 164.402; and any other information Covered Entity needs for its own
   notices under 45 CFR 164.404(c) and section 501.171, Florida Statutes
   (45 CFR 164.410(c)(2)).
6. **Manner.** Business Associate first notifies Covered Entity's contact in section 7(d) by
   telephone and then in writing. Written reports carry no PHI by email; the identification of
   Individuals is delivered through a secure channel Covered Entity designates.
7. **Notices to Individuals, the Secretary, and the media.** Covered Entity makes the
   notifications required of it under 45 CFR 164.404 to 164.408 and section 501.171, Florida
   Statutes. [On Covered Entity's written request, Business Associate drafts or sends those
   notifications on Covered Entity's behalf.] [ALLOCATION OF THE COSTS OF NOTIFICATION AND
   MITIGATION FOR A BREACH CAUSED BY BUSINESS ASSOCIATE OR ITS SUBCONTRACTOR.]

(d) In accordance with 45 CFR 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), ensure that any
Subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of Business Associate
agree in writing, by a contract or other arrangement that complies with 45 CFR 164.504(e) and
164.314(a), to the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements that apply to Business
Associate with respect to such information, including compliance with Subpart C of 45 CFR
Part 164. Business Associate keeps a list of those Subcontractors and gives it to Covered Entity
on request.

(e) Make available PHI in a Designated Record Set to Covered Entity as necessary to satisfy
Covered Entity's obligations under 45 CFR 164.524, within [NUMBER] business days of Covered
Entity's request. Business Associate forwards to Covered Entity, within [NUMBER] business days,
any request for access it receives directly from an Individual.

(f) Make any amendments to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed or agreed to by Covered
Entity under 45 CFR 164.526, or take other measures as necessary to satisfy Covered Entity's
obligations under 45 CFR 164.526, within [NUMBER] business days of Covered Entity's request.
Business Associate forwards to Covered Entity any request for amendment it receives directly from
an Individual.

(g) Maintain and make available to Covered Entity the information required to provide an
accounting of disclosures as necessary to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations under
45 CFR 164.528, within [NUMBER] business days of Covered Entity's request. Business Associate
forwards to Covered Entity any request for an accounting it receives directly from an Individual.

(h) To the extent Business Associate is to carry out one or more of Covered Entity's obligations
under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164, comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to
Covered Entity in the performance of such obligations.

(i) Make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI
available to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

## 3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures by Business Associate

(a) Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Services,
including receiving and storing appointment requests submitted through the patient website,
presenting them to Covered Entity's workforce in the staff portal, recording the actions and notes
of Covered Entity's workforce, and hosting, backing up, restoring, securing, monitoring, and
migrating the system that holds them. Business Associate may not de-identify PHI except as
Covered Entity directs in writing.

(b) Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as Required By Law.

(c) Business Associate agrees to make uses and disclosures and requests for PHI consistent with
Covered Entity's minimum necessary policies and procedures.

(d) Business Associate may not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate Subpart E of
45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity[, except for the specific uses and disclosures set out
in sections 3(e) and 3(f)].

(e) [OPTIONAL] Business Associate may use PHI for the proper management and administration of
Business Associate or to carry out the legal responsibilities of Business Associate.

(f) [OPTIONAL] Business Associate may disclose PHI for the proper management and administration
of Business Associate or to carry out the legal responsibilities of Business Associate, provided
the disclosures are Required By Law, or Business Associate obtains reasonable assurances from the
person to whom the information is disclosed that the information will remain confidential and be
used or further disclosed only as Required By Law or for the purposes for which it was disclosed
to the person, and the person notifies Business Associate of any instances of which it is aware in
which the confidentiality of the information has been breached.

(g) Business Associate does not provide Data Aggregation services under this Agreement, does not
sell PHI or use it for marketing, and does not use PHI to train or evaluate machine-learning
models.

## 4. Provisions for Covered Entity to Inform Business Associate of Privacy Practices and Restrictions

(a) Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitation in Covered Entity's Notice of
Privacy Practices under 45 CFR 164.520, to the extent that such limitation may affect Business
Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

(b) Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any changes in, or revocation of, the
permission by an Individual to use or disclose his or her PHI, to the extent that such changes may
affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

(c) Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any restriction on the use or disclosure of
PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to or is required to abide by under 45 CFR 164.522, to the
extent that such restriction may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

## 5. Permissible Requests by Covered Entity

Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that
would not be permissible under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity[, except as
section 3(e) or 3(f) allows].

## 6. Term and Termination

(a) **Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the date above and terminates when the Service
Agreement terminates or on the date Covered Entity terminates this Agreement for cause under
section 6(b), whichever is sooner.

(b) **Termination for cause.** Business Associate authorizes termination of this Agreement by
Covered Entity if Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has violated a material term
of this Agreement [and Business Associate has not cured the breach or ended the violation within
[NUMBER] days of Covered Entity's written notice].

(c) **Obligations of Business Associate upon termination.**

[Option 1, if sections 3(e) and 3(f) are not included:]

(1) Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, Business Associate shall return to Covered
Entity [or, if agreed to by Covered Entity, destroy] all PHI received from Covered Entity, or
created, maintained, or received by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity, that Business
Associate still maintains in any form, and shall retain no copies of it. Business Associate
returns PHI as an encrypted export in a format and through a channel the Parties agree. It
destroys PHI by deleting the database, its snapshots and every export, ensuring that each Subcontractor deletes
the PHI it holds, and scheduling the deletion of the encryption keys that protected it. Copies in
automated backups are destroyed when they reach the end of the backup retention period, which is
no more than thirty-five (35) days. Business Associate confirms the return or destruction to
Covered Entity in writing.

[Option 2, if sections 3(e) and 3(f) are included:]

(2) Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, Business Associate, with respect to PHI
received from Covered Entity, or created, maintained, or received by Business Associate on behalf
of Covered Entity, shall: (i) retain only that PHI which is necessary for Business Associate to
continue its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities;
(ii) return to Covered Entity [or, if agreed to by Covered Entity, destroy] the remaining PHI that
Business Associate still maintains in any form, as described in option 1; (iii) continue to use
appropriate safeguards and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 with respect to Electronic
Protected Health Information to prevent use or disclosure of the retained PHI, other than as
provided for in this section, for as long as Business Associate retains it; (iv) not use or
disclose the retained PHI other than for the purposes for which it was retained and subject to
the same conditions set out in sections 3(e) and 3(f) that applied before termination; and
(v) return to Covered Entity [or, if agreed to by Covered Entity, destroy] the retained PHI when it
is no longer needed for those purposes.

(3) If returning or destroying any PHI is not feasible, Business Associate notifies Covered Entity
of the conditions that make it infeasible, extends the protections of this Agreement to that PHI,
and limits further uses and disclosures to the purposes that make its return or destruction
infeasible, for as long as Business Associate maintains it.

(d) **Survival.** The obligations of Business Associate under this section 6 survive the
termination of this Agreement.

## 7. Miscellaneous

(a) **Regulatory references.** A reference in this Agreement to a section in the HIPAA Rules means
the section as in effect or as amended.

(b) **Amendment.** The Parties agree to take such action as is necessary to amend this Agreement
from time to time as is necessary for compliance with the requirements of the HIPAA Rules and any
other applicable law.

(c) **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity in this Agreement shall be interpreted to permit compliance
with the HIPAA Rules. If this Agreement and the Service Agreement conflict on the treatment of PHI,
this Agreement controls.
