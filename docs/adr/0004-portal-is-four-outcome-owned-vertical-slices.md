# The portal is four outcome-owned vertical slices

The staff portal remains one product, one `/admin` design surface, and one shared shell under the
Front Desk Ledger world. It is not one product outcome with subordinate pages. Product ownership
within it is divided among four end-to-end vertical slices: **Home** owns orientation, triage,
handoff, and the next useful action; **Appointments** owns the complete appointment-request
lifecycle and working queue; **Settings** owns staff access, notification recipients, and software
administration; and **Help** owns cross-job guidance, recovery beyond a slice's own path, and
transitions to human support.

A vertical slice is a complete staff outcome, not a route or page. Each slice is accountable for
its job, experience thesis, information architecture, state matrix, PHI-free instrumentation,
tests, and Product Experience acceptance. A route may contribute to an outcome without defining
the slice, and an ancillary utility does not become a fifth slice merely because it has a URL.

Navigation, authentication, authorization policy and enforcement, design tokens, and the Front
Desk Ledger world remain shared horizontal infrastructure. Settings exposes staff access
administration while authorization itself remains shared. The Activity log and review-flyer
printing stay named utilities placed with the outcomes they support. This decision establishes
product ownership boundaries; it does not move routes, change runtime behavior, or claim that
every slice-specific artifact is complete.
